TOPIC: Flood Safety

During flooding, people should avoid walking or driving through floodwater. Floodwater can conceal hazards and may move with dangerous force.

TOPIC: Evacuation

When authorities issue an evacuation order, people should follow official instructions and use designated evacuation routes. They should not enter flooded roads to reach a shelter.

TOPIC: Emergency Preparedness

People should monitor official weather and disaster management advisories. Emergency supplies may include drinking water, essential medicines, a flashlight and important contact information.

TOPIC: Emergency Response

Responders should verify reports, identify affected areas, consider population exposure, and check road accessibility before planning assistance. Unverified reports should not be treated as confirmed incidents.

TOPIC: Uncertainty

Satellite-derived flood extent, rainfall measurements and population exposure estimates have limitations. Emergency decisions should be checked against current official information and qualified responder assessments.

### Portal role
The SACHET portal disseminates area-specific disaster alerts from authorized alert-generating agencies and State/UT Disaster Management Authorities. It provides disaster-specific Do's and Don'ts and supports multiple Indian languages.

### Source guidance — Cyclone section
The supplied portal text lists the following cyclone-related guidance:

- Identify nearby safe high ground or a safe shelter and the safest route when a storm-surge, tide, or flooding warning is issued.
- Store ready-to-eat food and water for at least a week.
- Evacuate to safe places when directed by government officials.
- Keep animals untied to support their safety during a cyclone.

**Scope limitation:** These points were supplied under the portal's Cyclone section. Do not label them as a dedicated flood-safety checklist without verifying the corresponding flood-specific guidance.
